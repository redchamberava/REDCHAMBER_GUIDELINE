04 · Requirements

This one’s going up on your account, so it should look like you. These few things just help it land, and a good video keeps working for you long after you post it.

Required
  • ⭐Film in bright, natural daylight
  • ⭐Use more product than you would every day
    Camera and lighting flatten colour. Build it up until it reads on screen, not just in the mirror.
  • ⭐Keep the finish dewy
    Don’t set it with powder.
  • ⭐Label the shade name on screen when swatching
  • ⭐Voiceover or talking-head style both work
Do not
  • ❌Dim or warm-cast indoor light
  • ❌Heavy filters or colour grading
    Colour accuracy is the whole point.
  • ❌ “RED CHAMBER” in the cover text or title
    Keep it organic, not like an ad.
